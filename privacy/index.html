Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to the text and files you enter when you use HecTool. The short version: most tools run entirely in your browser and nothing leaves your device; the AI tools send only the text or prompt you enter to a third-party AI provider so it can generate a result; and no account data is collected, because there are no accounts.

Tools that run in your browser

All tools except the twenty AI tools run fully in your browser. Text, numbers, dates and files you enter are used only in the open browser session to produce the result you asked for. Files selected for image and PDF tools are never uploaded and never leave your device. Closing the page ends the session, and this site does not save your inputs or results online.

AI text and image tools

When you use an AI text or image tool, the text or prompt you enter — and the options you choose — is sent to the third-party AI provider via the site's /api/ai endpoint (Pollinations) so it can generate a result. The request travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and nothing is sent before you press the generate button. The AI provider receives only what you submit for that request. This page does not store your AI inputs or results; they exist only in the open browser session. Do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive information into AI tools. AI tools also limit how much text can be sent in a single request.

No accounts, no tracking

HecTool collects no account data, because it offers no accounts, sign-up or saved histories. It runs no analytics, sets no tracking cookies and uses no advertising trackers. The page contains no API keys, secrets or passwords — AI requests pass through a secure server endpoint, and the AI service used (Pollinations) requires no API key. All connections use HTTPS.

Third-party resources

Some tools load software libraries from a content delivery network: the QR and barcode generators, the PDF tools (pdf-lib and pdf.js), the Markdown, encryption and password-strength tools, and the currency converter, which fetches public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. Your files and content are processed locally and are not sent to the library providers. The What Is My IP tool attempts to fetch your public IP address from api.ipify.org; the site's security policy normally blocks that request, in which case no IP address is shown. The site also loads a font from Google Fonts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page. There is no published email address for this site, so the contact form is the way to reach the site owner.
