Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on HecTool is free to use — no account and no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available, so at very busy times one may ask you to try again in a little while.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open any tool and start using it right away.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Image files never leave your device — those tools run locally. For AI tools, the text you type is sent to the AI service so it can generate your result; do not enter passwords or other sensitive information.

How do the AI tools work?

Enter your text, choose your options and press the generate button — nothing is sent before that. Your request goes over a secure connection to our AI endpoint and the real AI-generated answer is shown. If the service is unavailable, you see a simple error instead of a made-up answer.

Which languages can the AI Translator handle?

Urdu, English, Arabic, Hindi, French, Spanish, German and Chinese — in any direction.

Does HecTool work on mobile?

Yes. The layout and controls are designed for phones first, and scale up cleanly to tablets and desktops.

Which formats do the image tools support?

JPG, PNG and WebP — for resizing, compressing and converting between formats.

Do the finance calculators give financial advice?

No. The calculators show arithmetic estimates from the numbers you enter. They are general utilities, not financial advice — check important decisions with a qualified professional.
