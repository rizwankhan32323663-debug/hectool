Business — 10 free online tools

Ten tools for small businesses and freelancers: send a clean invoice or receipt, find a brandable name and a slogan to go with it, sketch a simple logo and business card, and run the numbers on pricing, profit, break-even — and even the true cost of a meeting. No accounts and no watermarks: just the document, or the answer.

  • Invoice Generator — Build a clean invoice with line items, tax and totals — preview, print or download it as text.
  • Business Name Generator — Get 10 brandable business name ideas from a keyword and your industry.
  • Slogan Generator — Get 8 catchy slogans for your product or brand in the tone you pick.
  • Logo Maker — Draw a simple logo on canvas — your business name, a shape and two colors — then download it as a PNG.
  • Business Card Maker — Lay out a clean business card with your name, title and contact details, then download it as a PNG.
  • Price Calculator — Set a selling price from your cost and target margin, and see the profit per unit.
  • Profit Calculator — Revenue minus cost gives your profit and profit margin percentage.
  • Break-Even Calculator — Find how many units you must sell to cover fixed costs, and the revenue at break-even.
  • Meeting Cost Calculator — See what a meeting really costs from headcount, average hourly rate and duration.
  • Receipt Generator — Build a simple receipt with items, total and payment method — preview, print or download it.
