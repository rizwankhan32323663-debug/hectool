Contact HecTool

Have a tool suggestion, or found a problem with a tool? Use the form below. Your message goes to the site owner. There is no published email address for this site, so this form is the way to get in touch.

What helps most

When reporting a problem, include the tool name, your device or browser, and what you expected to happen. A short description of the steps that led to the problem is usually enough. For a tool suggestion, tell us the task you are trying to complete and what the tool should do — a concrete example helps more than a general idea. Please never send private documents, passwords or other sensitive information when asking for help with a tool.

Before you write

Many questions are answered on the page itself. The about page explains how the tools work, the privacy policy explains what happens to your data, and each tool page includes answers to common questions about that tool. If a tool is behaving oddly, try refreshing the page and entering your input again — browser tools run locally, so a fresh page usually clears a one-off glitch.

Send a message

Your message goes to the site owner. Please do not include passwords, private documents or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Will I get a reply?

Every message is read by the site owner. A personal reply is not always possible, especially for general suggestions, but reports of broken tools are acted on first.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. Describe the task the tool should solve and give an example of the input and the result you would expect. Suggestions that many people could use are the most likely to be built.

I found a bug. What should I include?

The tool name, your device and browser, the input you used, and what happened instead of what you expected. Screenshots described in words are fine; do not send private documents.
