Zakat Calculator

What is this tool?

The Zakat Calculator adds up cash, gold value, silver value, business assets and money owed to you, then subtracts liabilities and debts to give net zakatable wealth. If that wealth is at or above the nisab threshold you enter, Zakat due is 2.5% of it.

You enter the nisab value yourself. No nisab amount is prefilled and no live gold or silver price is used. The result is an educational estimate only, and you should consult a scholar for rulings on your own situation.

How to use Zakat Calculator

  1. Enter your cash, covering bank balances and cash in hand.
  2. Enter the values of your gold, silver and business assets or stock.
  3. Enter money owed to you and your liabilities or debts.
  4. Enter your nisab threshold in the Nisab threshold field.
  5. Tap Calculate Zakat and read your net zakatable wealth and the Zakat due.

Is it free?

Yes. The Zakat Calculator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Educational estimate only; it is not a religious ruling.
  • No live gold or silver prices are used, so you must enter your own nisab value.
  • It uses only the values you enter and does not cover every asset type or ruling detail.

Alternatives

  • Islamic Relief (islamic-relief.org) — offers a Zakat calculator.
  • National Zakat Foundation (nzf.org.uk) — offers a Zakat calculator.

Example (illustrative)

Input: cash 500, gold value 800, silver value 50, business assets 200, money owed to you 100, liabilities 150, nisab 1,000. Output (illustrative): net zakatable wealth 1,500, Zakat due 37.50 at 2.5%.

Frequently asked questions

What rate does the calculator use?

It uses 2.5% of net zakatable wealth when wealth is at or above the nisab you enter.

What is nisab?

Nisab is the minimum threshold of zakatable wealth above which Zakat is due. You enter the threshold value yourself in this tool.

Why is no nisab value prefilled?

Nisab values change with gold and silver prices, and this tool uses no live prices, so it does not claim a current nisab. Enter the value you are using.

What if my wealth is below nisab?

Zakat due is shown as 0, with an explanation that your net zakatable wealth is below the nisab you entered.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the values you enter stay on your device.
