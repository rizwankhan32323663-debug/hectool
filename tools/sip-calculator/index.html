SIP Calculator

What is this tool?

The SIP Calculator estimates how regular monthly investments could grow. Enter a monthly investment, an expected annual return and a number of years, and it estimates the future value, the total invested and the estimated gains.

It also shows a year-by-year table and bars so you can see how the estimated value builds over time. The results are estimates based on a steady monthly amount and a steady return, not a promise of what will happen.

How to use SIP Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly investment amount.
  2. Enter the expected annual return as a percentage.
  3. Enter the number of years.
  4. Tap Calculate estimate.
  5. Read the estimated future value, total invested and estimated gains, then check the year-by-year table and growth bars.

Is it free?

Yes. The SIP Calculator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Results are estimates only; actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.
  • Fees, taxes and inflation are not included.
  • It assumes the same monthly amount and the same steady return in every month.

Frequently asked questions

What is an SIP?

An SIP is a way of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually every month. This tool estimates growth for a fixed monthly amount.

Is the result guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate. Actual returns vary with markets and the investment chosen.

What happens if I enter a 0% return?

The estimated future value equals the total invested, because no growth is added.

Does it include fees or tax?

No. Fees, taxes and inflation are not included in the estimate.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the numbers you enter stay on your device.
