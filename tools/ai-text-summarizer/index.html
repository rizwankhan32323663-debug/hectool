AI Text Summarizer

What is this tool?

The AI Text Summarizer condenses long text — articles, notes, reports, documents — into a short, medium, or detailed summary, in paragraph or bullet-point style. It is for getting the gist of long reading quickly. It does not verify facts: summaries can omit nuance or misrepresent the source, so check important claims against the original.

How to use AI Text Summarizer

  1. In the Text to summarize field, paste the full text (placeholder: "Paste the long article, notes or document text here…"). The field accepts up to 12,000 characters.
  2. Choose a Summary length: Short (a few sentences), Medium (one paragraph), or Detailed (key points covered).
  3. Choose a Style: Paragraph or Bullet points.
  4. Press Summarize. Your text is sent to the AI service only when you press the button.
  5. Read the result under "Your AI summary". Use Copy result, Download .txt, or Clear / Reset.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Text Summarizer is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • Summaries can omit nuance, drop caveats, or misrepresent the source. Check important claims against the original text.
  • The input field is limited to 12,000 characters; longer documents must be split.
  • Longer source text gives the AI more to work with — very short inputs produce thin summaries.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail.

Alternatives

  • SmallSEOTools Summarizing Tool (smallseotools.com) — a summarizing tool with a free tier.
  • QuillBot Summarizer (quillbot.com) — a summarizing tool with a free tier.

Example (illustrative)

Input: A ~2,000-word article about the benefits of regular exercise. Output (illustrative, Medium, Paragraph): A one-paragraph summary naming the article's main benefits — energy, focus, sleep, and long-term health — without its detail or caveats. Actual wording varies per generation.

Frequently asked questions

How long can the source text be?

Up to 12,000 characters per request. Longer documents must be split.

Which length should I choose?

Short for a quick gist, Medium for one paragraph, Detailed when you need the key points covered.

Paragraph or bullet points?

Paragraph reads as prose; bullet points scan faster. Pick the style that fits how you will use it.

Is the summary accurate?

Usually close, but summaries can omit nuance or misrepresent the source. Verify important claims against the original.
