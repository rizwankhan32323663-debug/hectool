Currency Converter

What is this tool?

Currency Converter converts an amount from one currency to another using live public exchange rates, fetched when the tool opens. It is for travel estimates, shopping comparisons, and rough conversions. Rates are public estimates for general information — not financial advice, and not the rate a bank or exchange desk will actually give you.

How to use Currency Converter

  1. In the Amount field, enter the amount to convert.
  2. Choose the source currency in the From dropdown.
  3. Choose the target currency in the To dropdown.
  4. Press Convert.
  5. Read the converted amount and the rate shown.

Is it free?

Yes. Currency Converter is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Rates are live public estimates from open.er-api.com. They can differ from bank, card, or exchange-desk rates, which include fees and spreads.
  • Rates are fetched when the tool opens. If they cannot load, the tool shows an honest message instead of a stale rate.
  • This tool is not financial advice.

Alternatives

  • XE Currency Converter (xe.com) — a free currency converter with live rates.
  • Wise Currency Converter (wise.com) — a free converter with mid-market rates.

Example (illustrative)

Input: Amount 1, From USD, To PKR. Output (illustrative): The converted amount at the live public rate shown by the tool. Rates change constantly, so no fixed figure is stated here.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the rates come from?

Live public rates from open.er-api.com, fetched when the tool opens.

Why does my bank give a different rate?

Banks and exchange desks add fees and spreads. This tool shows a public mid-market-style estimate, not a dealing rate.

What if rates fail to load?

The tool shows an honest message instead of a stale rate. Reload the page to try again.

Is my amount stored?

No. Conversion runs in your browser; nothing is stored.

Can I convert crypto?

This tool converts fiat currencies listed in its dropdowns. ---
