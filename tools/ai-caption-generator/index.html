AI Caption Generator

What is this tool?

The AI Caption Generator writes social-media captions with hashtags for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Describe your post, pick the platform and tone, and get caption options to edit and post. It is a drafting aid — always check names, dates, offers, and claims before posting.

How to use AI Caption Generator

  1. In the Post topic or description field, describe your post (placeholder: "e.g. Launching our new handmade leather wallet collection this Friday"). The field accepts up to 1,200 characters.
  2. Choose a Platform: Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok.
  3. Choose a Tone: Engaging, Funny, Professional, or Inspirational.
  4. Press Generate captions. Your text is sent to the AI service only when you press the button.
  5. Read the caption options under "Your AI captions + hashtags". Edit names, dates, and offers before posting. Use Copy result, Download .txt, or Clear / Reset.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Caption Generator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • AI captions can invent details (dates, prices, names). Verify every fact before posting.
  • Captions are drafts — platform rules, character limits, and hashtag norms still apply.
  • The input field is limited to 1,200 characters.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail.

Alternatives

  • TinyWow AI Write (tinywow.com) — offers caption-style AI writing with a free tier.
  • Hootsuite AI Caption Generator (hootsuite.com) — generates captions with a free tier.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "Launching our new handmade leather wallet collection this Friday", Platform Instagram, Tone Engaging. Output (illustrative): Three caption options with hashtags, e.g. a launch announcement with a call to action and relevant hashtags. Actual wording varies per generation.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms does it support?

Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

Does it include hashtags?

Yes — caption options come with hashtags. Edit them to match your post.

Should I post the caption as-is?

No. Check names, dates, offers, and claims first — AI can invent details.

Is my description stored?

No. It is sent to the AI service only when you press Generate captions, and it is not stored by the page.

Why did generation fail?

The AI service was unavailable at that moment. Try again in a little while. ---
