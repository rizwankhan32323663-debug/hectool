IBAN Validator

What is this tool?

IBAN Validator checks whether an International Bank Account Number (IBAN) is correctly formed: its format, its length for its country, and its mod-97 checksum.

It does not verify that the account exists or belongs to anyone. The check happens in your browser.

How to use IBAN Validator

  1. Enter the IBAN in the IBAN box, with or without spaces, or tap Use example IBAN.
  2. Tap Validate IBAN.
  3. Read the formatted IBAN, country code, expected length, actual length and checksum result.
  4. Read the verdict: valid only when the format, length and checksum all pass.

Is it free?

Yes. IBAN Validator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • A valid result means the format and checksum pass; it does not verify that the account exists.
  • Country lengths are checked only for the countries in the built-in table; for other country codes only the checksum is checked.
  • India (IN) and the United States (US) do not use IBAN, so codes starting with IN or US cannot be valid IBANs.

Alternatives

  • IBAN.com (iban.com) — checks IBAN format and checksum.
  • Wise (wise.com) — offers an IBAN checker.

Example (illustrative)

Input: GB29 NWBK 6016 1331 9268 19. Output (illustrative): formatted IBAN GB29 NWBK 6016 1331 9268 19, length 22 as expected for GB, checksum pass, verdict valid. Changing the last digit to 8 makes the checksum fail.

Frequently asked questions

What does the IBAN check cover?

The format, the length expected for the country code, and the mod-97 checksum, which must equal 1.

Does a valid result mean the account exists?

No. It means the IBAN is correctly formed. It does not verify that the account exists or belongs to anyone.

What if my country is not in the table?

The tool says the country is not in the table and checks the checksum only; the length cannot be confirmed.

Which countries do not use IBAN?

Many countries do not use IBAN, including India (IN) and the United States (US). IBANs starting with those codes are reported as not valid IBANs.

Is my IBAN sent anywhere?

No. The check happens in your browser and the IBAN is not sent or stored anywhere.
