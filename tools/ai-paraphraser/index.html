AI Paraphraser / Rewriter

What is this tool?

The AI Paraphraser rewrites text in different words while keeping the original meaning. It is for rephrasing sentences, simplifying dense writing, varying wording in drafts, or polishing a paragraph. It does not check facts — always review the result, because paraphrasing can subtly shift meaning.

How to use AI Paraphraser / Rewriter

  1. In the Text to paraphrase field, paste the paragraph or text you want rewritten (placeholder: "Paste the paragraph or text you want rewritten in different words…"). The field accepts up to 12,000 characters.
  2. Choose a Style: Simple (clear and easy), Formal (polished and professional), Creative (fresh and expressive), Casual (natural and relaxed), or Fluent (smooth and natural).
  3. Choose a Rewrite strength: Light (small changes), Medium (clear rewording), or Strong (fully rephrased).
  4. Press Paraphrase text. Your text is sent to the AI service only when you press the button.
  5. Read the result under "Your paraphrased text". Use Copy result, Download .txt, or Clear / Reset.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Paraphraser is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • Paraphrasing can subtly change meaning, tone, or emphasis. Always compare the result against the original.
  • AI output can be inaccurate or awkward. Review before using.
  • The input field is limited to 12,000 characters.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog." Output (illustrative, Style Simple, Strength Medium): "The fast brown fox leaps over the sleepy dog." Actual wording varies per generation.

Frequently asked questions

Does paraphrasing change the meaning?

It should not, but it can — subtly. Always compare the result with the original before using it.

What is the difference between Light, Medium, and Strong?

Light makes small changes, Medium clearly rewords, Strong fully rephrases. Stronger rewrites carry more risk of meaning drift.

Can I paraphrase someone else's text and call it mine?

No. Paraphrasing does not transfer authorship or rights. Cite sources and follow your institution's or publisher's rules.

Is my text stored?

No. It is sent to the AI service only when you press Paraphrase text, and it is not stored by the page.

Why did paraphrasing fail?

The AI service was unavailable at that moment. Try again in a little while. ---
