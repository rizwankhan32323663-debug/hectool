AI Image Generator

What is this tool?

The AI Image Generator turns a text description into a picture. Describe what you want — a scene, an object, a character, a wallpaper — pick a style and size, and the tool generates an image you can download. It is for illustrations, thumbnails, wallpapers, concept art, and social posts. It does not reproduce real photographs or specific copyrighted characters.

How to use AI Image Generator

  1. In the Describe the image field, write a clear description (placeholder: "e.g. A golden dragon flying over a misty mountain lake at sunrise").
  2. Choose a Style: Photorealistic, Anime, 3D Render, Watercolor, Cyberpunk, Minimalist, Cinematic, or Oil Painting.
  3. Choose a Size: Square (1024 × 1024), Wide (1280 × 720), or Portrait (768 × 1024).
  4. Press Generate Image. The picture is generated online and shown on the page.
  5. Press Download Image to save it.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Image Generator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. Image generation depends on the image service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • Generated images can contain distortions (hands, faces, text in images) and will not match a description exactly.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail.
  • Do not ask it to reproduce real people, logos, or copyrighted characters, and do not present generated images as real photographs.

Alternatives

  • TinyWow AI Image Generator (tinywow.com) — offers an AI image generator with a free tier.
  • Microsoft Bing Image Creator (bing.com/images/create) — generates images with a free tier; requires a Microsoft account.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "A golden dragon flying over a misty mountain lake at sunrise", Style Cinematic, Size Wide (1280 × 720). Output (illustrative): A wide cinematic-style image of a golden dragon above a misty lake at sunrise. The actual image varies per generation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the generated images?

You may use the images you generate, but you are responsible for how you use them — do not present them as real photographs or use them to mislead.

Why does the image look distorted?

AI image models can struggle with hands, faces, and text. Try rephrasing the description or regenerating.

How long does generation take?

Usually seconds to under a minute. At busy times it can take longer or fail; try again.

Is my description stored?

Your description is sent to the image service to generate the picture. The page does not store your descriptions.

Can it make logos or avatars?

Yes — describe the logo or avatar you want. HecTool also has dedicated AI Logo Generator and AI Avatar Maker tools. ---
