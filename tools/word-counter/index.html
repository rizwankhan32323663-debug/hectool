Word Counter

What is this tool?

Word Counter counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs in any text you type or paste, and estimates reading time. It is for essays, articles, social posts, emails, and anything with a word limit. Counts update instantly as you type.

How to use Word Counter

  1. Click into the Your text field (placeholder: "Start typing or paste text here…").
  2. Type or paste your text. Counts update instantly — no button needed.
  3. Read the counts: words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time.
  4. Use Copy text to copy your text back out.

Is it free?

Yes. Word Counter is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Reading time uses an average of 200 words per minute — a rough estimate, not a measurement of any individual reader.
  • Counts follow the tool's counting rules; other tools may count hyphenated words or numbers slightly differently.

Alternatives

  • WordCounter.net (wordcounter.net) — a free word and character counter.
  • SmallSEOTools Word Counter (smallseotools.com) — a free word counter.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "Hello world. This is a test." (6 words, 28 characters, 23 without spaces, 2 sentences, 1 paragraph). Output (illustrative): Words 6, Chars 28, No-spaces 23, Sentences 2, Paragraphs 1, Reading ~1 min.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count words as I type?

Yes — counts update instantly, with no button to press.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time uses an average of 200 words per minute. It is an estimate.

Are spaces counted?

Both totals are shown: characters including spaces, and characters without spaces.

Is my text stored or uploaded?

No. Counting runs in your browser; your text never leaves your device.

Why does another counter show a different number?

Counting rules differ (hyphens, numbers, line breaks). Small differences between tools are normal. ---
