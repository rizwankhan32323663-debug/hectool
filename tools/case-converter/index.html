Case Converter

What is this tool?

Case Converter changes the letter case of any text — to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, or snake_case. It is for fixing headings, formatting identifiers, normalising pasted text, or converting between naming styles. Conversion updates live as you type.

How to use Case Converter

  1. In the Your text field, type or paste your text (placeholder: "Type or paste text here…").
  2. Press a case button: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, or snake_case. Conversion updates live.
  3. Read the result in the converted-text field (placeholder: "Your converted text appears here").
  4. Press Copy result to copy it, or Clear to start over.

Is it free?

Yes. Case Converter is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Case conversion is mechanical — it does not fix spelling, grammar, or proper nouns.
  • Title Case and Sentence case follow the tool's rules; style guides differ on which words to capitalise.

Alternatives

  • Convert Case (convertcase.net) — a free case converter.
  • SmallSEOTools Case Converter (smallseotools.com) — a free case converter.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "hello world". Output (illustrative): UPPERCASE → "HELLO WORLD"; Title Case → "Hello World"; camelCase → "helloWorld"; snake_case → "hello_world".

Frequently asked questions

Does it convert as I type?

Yes — conversion updates live as you type.

What is camelCase?

First word lowercase, each following word capitalised, no spaces — e.g. "helloWorld". Common in programming identifiers.

What is snake_case?

All lowercase with underscores between words — e.g. "hello_world". Common in programming identifiers.

Is my text stored?

No. Conversion runs in your browser; your text never leaves your device.

Does it fix grammar?

No. It only changes letter case. ---
