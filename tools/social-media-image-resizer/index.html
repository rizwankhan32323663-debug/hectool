Social Media Image Resizer

What is this tool?

The Social Media Image Resizer resizes an image to common platform sizes in your browser. Presets cover Instagram Post 1080 x 1080, Instagram Story 1080 x 1920, YouTube Thumbnail 1280 x 720, YouTube Banner 2560 x 1440, X Header 1500 x 500 and Facebook Cover 820 x 312, and a custom size option takes any width and height you enter.

Fit mode keeps the whole image inside the target size with a background color or transparency, while Fill mode scales the image to cover the size and crops the centre. Output can be JPG, PNG or WebP, with a quality slider for JPG and WebP. The live preview shows the resized image, and the output dimensions and actual file size are reported honestly.

How to use Social Media Image Resizer

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image, up to 20 MB.
  2. Choose a size preset, such as Instagram Post, Instagram Story, YouTube Thumbnail, YouTube Banner, X Header or Facebook Cover, or choose Custom size and enter your own width and height.
  3. Choose Fit to keep the whole image with a background, or Fill to cover the size and crop the edges.
  4. Choose the output format, background color or transparency, and quality for JPG and WebP.
  5. Check the live preview and the reported output dimensions and file size, then tap Download image.

Is it free?

Yes. The Social Media Image Resizer is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Input is limited to JPG, PNG or WebP files up to 20 MB; HEIC images are not supported.
  • JPG output has no transparency, so a transparent background becomes white in JPG.
  • The quality slider applies to JPG and WebP only; PNG output ignores it.
  • Very large custom dimensions may fail to render on some devices; the tool reports an error instead of a partial result.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Fit and Fill?

Fit keeps the whole image inside the target size and fills the empty space with the background color, so nothing is cropped. Fill scales the image to cover the whole target size and crops the edges that fall outside it.

What happens to a transparent background in JPG?

JPG has no transparency, so a transparent background becomes white in JPG output. Use PNG or WebP if you need transparency.

Does the quality slider affect PNG output?

No. PNG ignores the quality slider. Quality applies to JPG and WebP output only.

Is my original image changed?

No. The original file is not changed. The resized image is a new file you download, and the original dimensions are shown next to the preview.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and your image stays on your device.
