Billable Hours Tracker

What is this tool?

The Billable Hours Tracker logs client work by hours and hourly rate. Add an entry for each piece of work and the amount is worked out automatically, with totals per client and a grand total of hours and amounts.

Entries are saved in your browser so they are still there when you come back on the same device, and you can export them as a CSV file for a spreadsheet. The tool does not create invoices and does not send your work anywhere.

How to use Billable Hours Tracker

  1. Enter the client, task, hours and hourly rate.
  2. Choose Add entry.
  3. Repeat for each piece of work; the amount for each entry is worked out automatically.
  4. Read the totals per client and the grand total.
  5. Use Remove on any entry you no longer need, Export CSV to download your entries, or Clear saved data to delete everything saved in this browser.

Is it free?

Yes. The Billable Hours Tracker is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Entries are saved in this browser only; clearing browser data may remove them.
  • Each entry is limited to 24 hours; split longer work into more than one entry.
  • It logs hours and amounts only; it does not create invoices or handle tax.
  • There is no edit button; remove an entry and add it again to correct it.

Alternatives

  • Toggl Track (toggl.com) — offers time tracking with billable hours.
  • Clockify (clockify.me) — offers time tracking with billable hours.

Example (illustrative)

Input: client Acme Studio, task Logo design, 2.5 hours, rate 40; then client Acme Studio, task Flyer, 1 hour, rate 40. Output (illustrative): two entries, Acme Studio total 3.5 hours and 140.00, grand total 3.5 hours and 140.00, in the CSV export.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my entries saved?

In your browser, using local storage. They stay on this device and in this browser, and clearing your browser data may remove them.

What does the CSV export contain?

One row per entry, with client, task, hours, rate and amount, in a format you can open in a spreadsheet.

Can I edit an entry after adding it?

No. Remove the entry and add it again with the corrected details.

Why is an entry limited to 24 hours?

The limit is per entry, so longer work should be split into more than one entry, for example one entry per day.

Is my work uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and your entries stay on your device.
