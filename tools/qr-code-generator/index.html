QR Code Generator

What is this tool?

QR Code Generator turns any text or URL into a scannable QR code you can download as a PNG. It is for sharing links, Wi-Fi details, contact info, menus, or any short text on print or screen. Your QR content is processed locally — it is not sent to anyone.

How to use QR Code Generator

  1. In the Text or URL field, enter a URL or any text (placeholder: "https://example.com or any text").
  2. Choose a QR size: 192 × 192, 256 × 256, 384 × 384, or 512 × 512.
  3. Press Generate QR code.
  4. Check the QR code shown. Press Download PNG to save it.

Is it free?

Yes. QR Code Generator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Very long text makes denser QR codes that are harder to scan, especially at small sizes or in print.
  • QR codes do not expire, but a QR code pointing to a URL stops being useful if that URL stops working.
  • Always test important QR codes with your camera before publishing.

Alternatives

  • QR Code Generator (qr-code-generator.com) — generates QR codes with a free tier.
  • TinyWow QR Generator (tinywow.com) — generates QR codes with a free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code expire?

No. The code itself does not expire. If it encodes a URL, it keeps working as long as that URL works.

What size should I choose?

256 × 256 suits most screens. Use 384 or 512 for print, posters, or long URLs.

Is my QR content sent anywhere?

No. Your QR content is processed locally and is not sent to anyone.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Try a larger size, higher print contrast, and a shorter URL. Test with your phone camera before publishing.

Can I make a QR code for Wi-Fi?

The tool encodes any text or URL. Wi-Fi QR codes need a specific text format — check your device's expected format before using it. ---
