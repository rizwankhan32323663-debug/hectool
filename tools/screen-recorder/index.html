Screen Recorder

What is this tool?

Screen Recorder records your screen, a window or a browser tab using your browser's screen capture, and lets you download the recording as a video file.

The recording stays on your device: it is previewed in the page and downloaded from your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

How to use Screen Recorder

  1. Tap Start recording and choose a screen, window or tab to share in the browser prompt.
  2. Watch the timer and status text while recording.
  3. Tap Stop when you are finished, or stop sharing from the browser prompt.
  4. Preview the recording in the Recording preview player.
  5. Tap Download video to save the recording as a WebM file.

Is it free?

Yes. Screen Recorder is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • Screen capture needs a browser that supports it and your permission; if permission is denied, recording cannot start.
  • Some browsers or tabs may not share audio, even when audio is requested.
  • Recordings are downloaded as WebM files and are not saved on this site.

Alternatives

  • OBS Studio (obsproject.com) — free screen recording software.
  • Loom (loom.com) — records screen videos in a browser.

Frequently asked questions

What can I record?

You can record your whole screen, a single window or a browser tab, depending on the options your browser offers when you tap Start recording.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. The recording stays on your device, is previewed in the page, and is downloaded from your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What file format is the download?

The recording is downloaded as a WebM video file named hectool-screen-recording.webm.

Why does recording not start?

Screen capture may not be supported in your browser, or permission may have been denied. Allow screen sharing and try again.

Can I record without giving permission?

No. Your browser asks you to choose what to share and to allow it; without that permission, recording cannot start.
