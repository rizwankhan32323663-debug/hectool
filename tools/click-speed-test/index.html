Click Speed Test

What is this tool?

The Click Speed Test measures how many times you can click in 10 seconds. Press Start, click the big button as fast as you can, and when time ends you get your total clicks, your clicks-per-second (CPS) score and a playful rating.

Only clicks during the 10 seconds count. Clicks before you press Start and after time ends are ignored, so every run starts from zero.

How to use Click Speed Test

  1. Press Start.
  2. Click the big Click here button as fast as you can for 10 seconds.
  3. Watch the countdown and the live click count.
  4. Read your clicks-per-second score and rating when the time ends.
  5. Press Restart to clear the score and try again.

Is it free?

Yes. The Click Speed Test is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • The test is fixed at 10 seconds.
  • Only clicks during the test count; clicks before Start and after the end are ignored.
  • Your mouse, touch screen or device can affect your score.
  • Rating labels are playful and are not a measurement standard.

Alternatives

  • ClickSpeedTest (clickspeedtest.com) — offers a click speed test.
  • Human Benchmark (humanbenchmark.com) — offers speed and reaction tests.

Example (illustrative)

Input: 62 clicks in 10 seconds. Output (illustrative): 6.2 clicks per second, rating Very fast.

Frequently asked questions

How is clicks per second calculated?

Total clicks during the test divided by 10 seconds. For example, 62 clicks gives 6.2 clicks per second.

What do the ratings mean?

Under 4 clicks per second is Steady, 4 to under 6 is Quick, 6 to under 8 is Very fast, and 8 or more is Lightning. The labels are playful.

Do clicks before I press Start count?

No. Clicks only count after you press Start and before the 10 seconds end.

Can I play on a touch screen?

Yes. Taps on the button count as clicks.

Is anything uploaded or recorded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and your score stays on your device.
