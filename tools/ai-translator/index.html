AI Translator

What is this tool?

The AI Translator translates text between eight languages: English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, French, Spanish, German, and Chinese — in any direction. It is for quick translations of sentences, paragraphs, messages, and documents. It is a general AI translation, not a certified or professional translation service.

How to use AI Translator

  1. In the Text to translate field, type or paste your text (placeholder: "Type or paste the text here…"). The field accepts up to 5,000 characters.
  2. Choose the source language in the From dropdown (default: English).
  3. Choose the target language in the To dropdown (default: Urdu).
  4. Use ⇄ Swap languages to flip the direction instantly.
  5. Press Translate. Your text is sent to the AI service only when you press the button.
  6. Read the result under "Your AI translation". Use Copy result, Download .txt, or Clear / Reset.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Translator is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • AI translation can mistranslate idioms, names, technical terms, and tone. Do not use it for legal, medical, or other high-stakes documents without a human check.
  • The input field is limited to 5,000 characters; longer texts must be split.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail with a simple error.

Alternatives

  • Google Translate (translate.google.com) — free translation between many languages.
  • DeepL Translator (deepl.com) — free translation tier with limits.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages does the AI Translator support?

Urdu, English, Arabic, Hindi, French, Spanish, German, and Chinese — in any direction.

Can it translate a whole document?

It accepts up to 5,000 characters per request. Longer documents must be split into parts.

Is the translation accurate?

It is a general AI translation. It is usually understandable but can err on idioms and technical terms — verify important translations.

Is my text stored?

No. It is sent to the AI service only when you press Translate, and it is not stored by the page.

Why did translation fail?

The AI service was unavailable at that moment. Try again in a little while. ---
