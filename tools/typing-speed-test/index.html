Typing Speed Test

What is this tool?

The Typing Speed Test gives you 60 seconds to type a short passage in English or Urdu. It reports words per minute (WPM), accuracy as a percentage, correct characters and total characters typed.

Scoring compares what you typed with the passage character by character, including spaces and punctuation. The timer starts on your first keystroke after you press Start, and typing is disabled when the 60 seconds end.

How to use Typing Speed Test

  1. Choose English or Urdu in the Language field.
  2. Press Start.
  3. Type the passage in the Type here box; the 60-second timer starts on your first character.
  4. Keep typing until the time ends.
  5. Read your WPM, accuracy, correct characters and total typed.
  6. Press Restart for a new passage.

Is it free?

Yes. The Typing Speed Test is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • The test is fixed at 60 seconds.
  • It uses a small set of short practice passages.
  • Accuracy compares characters exactly, so a missed space or punctuation mark counts as incorrect.
  • Your keyboard and layout can affect your result.

Alternatives

  • Monkeytype (monkeytype.com) — offers a typing test.
  • Typing.com (typing.com) — offers typing lessons and tests.

Example (illustrative)

Input: in 60 seconds you type 160 characters and 150 of them match the passage. Output (illustrative): WPM 30, accuracy 93.8%, correct characters 150 of 160.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

WPM is correct characters divided by 5, divided by minutes elapsed. In a full 60-second test, 150 correct characters gives 30 WPM.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is correct characters divided by total characters typed, compared with the passage character by character.

What do I need to type Urdu?

Use an Urdu keyboard or input method on your device. The test compares the characters you type with the Urdu passage exactly.

Can I restart with a new passage?

Yes. Press Restart to load a new passage from the language you chose.

Is my typing uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and what you type stays on your device.
