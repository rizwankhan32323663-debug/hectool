AI Grammar Checker

What is this tool?

The AI Grammar Checker corrects grammar, spelling, and punctuation in any text you paste. It returns the corrected version, and optionally a brief list of the fixes it made. It is for emails, essays, posts, and drafts. It is a writing aid, not a substitute for careful proofreading — it can miss errors and occasionally "fix" something that was correct.

How to use AI Grammar Checker

  1. In the Text to check field, paste your text (placeholder: "Paste your text here — grammar, spelling and punctuation will be fixed…"). The field accepts up to 12,000 characters.
  2. Choose a Result detail: "Corrected text only" or "Corrected text + brief list of fixes".
  3. Press Check & fix grammar. Your text is sent to the AI service only when you press the button.
  4. Read the result under "Your corrected text". Use Copy result, Download .txt, or Clear / Reset.

Is it free?

Yes. The AI Grammar Checker is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment. AI tools depend on the AI service being available; at very busy times one may ask you to try again.

Limitations

  • AI grammar checking can miss errors, misread intent, and occasionally change correct text.
  • It does not check facts, logic, or style consistency.
  • The input field is limited to 12,000 characters.
  • Generation can be slow and can occasionally fail.

Alternatives

  • Grammarly (grammarly.com) — a grammar checker with a free tier; full use requires an account.
  • LanguageTool (languagetool.org) — a grammar checker with a free tier.

Example (illustrative)

Input: "Their going to they're house tommorow." Output (illustrative, "Corrected text + brief list of fixes"): "They're going to their house tomorrow." + a brief list noting the their/they're/their correction and the spelling fix. Actual output varies.

Frequently asked questions

What does it fix?

Grammar, spelling, and punctuation. It does not check facts or style.

What does the "explained" option do?

"Ccorrected text + brief list of fixes" returns the corrected text plus a short bullet list of what changed.

Is it reliable?

It catches common errors but can miss things and can change correct text. Proofread important writing yourself.

Is my text stored?

No. It is sent to the AI service only when you press the button, and it is not stored by the page.

Why did checking fail?

The AI service was unavailable at that moment. Try again in a little while. ---
