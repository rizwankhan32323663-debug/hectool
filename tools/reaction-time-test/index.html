Reaction Time Test

What is this tool?

The Reaction Time Test measures how quickly you respond when the screen turns green. Each round waits a random 1 to 4 seconds while the area is red, then turns green, and your tap time is recorded in milliseconds.

You play 5 rounds. Tapping while the area is still red is too early, so that round is void and you retry it. At the end you get each round time, your average and your best time.

How to use Reaction Time Test

  1. Press Start to begin round 1 of 5.
  2. Wait while the area is red and do not tap.
  3. Tap the area as soon as it turns green.
  4. Repeat for all 5 rounds; a too-early tap voids the round and you retry it.
  5. Read each round time, your average and your best time.

Is it free?

Yes. The Reaction Time Test is free on HecTool — no account, no sign-up, no payment.

Limitations

  • The test is fixed at 5 rounds.
  • Tapping too early voids the round, so it must be retried.
  • Results depend on your device, screen and input delay and are not a laboratory measurement.
  • The waiting time is random between 1 and 4 seconds in each round.

Alternatives

Frequently asked questions

Why is the waiting time random?

A random wait between 1 and 4 seconds stops you from guessing the exact moment the screen turns green.

What happens if I tap too early?

The round is void and does not count. Press Start to retry that round.

How are the average and best times worked out?

The average is the mean of the 5 round times, and the best is the fastest single round.

Is the result exact?

No. Timing uses your device clock, and screen and input delays can affect the result, so treat it as a guide.

Is anything uploaded or recorded?

No. The tool runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and your round times stay on your device.
