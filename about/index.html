About HecTool

HecTool is a free collection of 200 online tools for everyday digital tasks. It covers calculators, converters, text utilities, image tools, developer tools, colour tools, date and time tools, health and daily tools, generators, PDF tools, audio tools, social media tools, student tools and business tools, plus twenty tools powered by artificial intelligence (AI). Every tool is free to use, with no account, no sign-up and no payment.

What you can do here

The 169 browser tools run entirely on your own device. They count words, resize and compress images, convert units and file formats, generate QR codes and passwords, calculate percentages, loans and taxes, and merge, split and rotate PDF files. Because the work happens in your browser, the files you select for image and PDF tools never leave your device. The ten AI text tools write, summarise, translate, paraphrase and check text, and the ten AI image tools generate pictures, avatars, logos and artwork from a written prompt. For those tools, only the text or prompt you enter is sent to the AI service so it can generate a result, through the site's /api/ai endpoint (Pollinations). Nothing is sent before you press the generate button.

Built to be simple

There is no dashboard, no saved history and no settings to learn. Open a tool, enter your text or file, choose any options and run it. Results appear straight away for browser tools, and within seconds for AI tools, which depend on the AI service being available. Copy the result or download it, and move on. The layout is designed for phones first and scales up to tablets and desktops, so the same tool works wherever you open it.

Who it is for

Students use the calculators, citation and study tools for coursework. Creators use the AI writing tools, hashtag and caption generators and image tools for posts and videos. Small businesses use the invoice, receipt, price and break-even tools for day-to-day paperwork. And anyone with a quick job — a unit conversion, a PDF to merge, a password to generate — can do it here without installing an app or creating an account.

Honest limits

Browser tools are limited only by your device and browser. AI tools depend on a third-party service, so at very busy times one may ask you to try again in a little while, and AI output should always be checked before you rely on it. Calculator results are arithmetic estimates from the numbers you enter, not professional advice. If you spot a problem or have an idea for a tool, tell the site owner through the contact page. You can also read how your data is handled in the privacy policy.
