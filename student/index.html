Student — 9 free online tools

Nine tools for school and self-study: format citations in APA or MLA, focus in 25-minute Pomodoro sessions, drill question-and-answer flashcards, check how readable an essay is, work out weighted grades, and spread study hours across the week. They run in the browser with no account, so they work the same on a school computer as they do at home.

  • Citation Generator — Format a citation in APA or MLA style from author, title, year and source.
  • Pomodoro Timer — Focus for 25 minutes, break for 5, with a long break after every 4 sessions.
  • Flashcard Maker — Build question and answer flashcards, flip them, and step through the deck.
  • Readability Checker — Check Flesch-Kincaid grade, Flesch reading ease, and word, sentence and syllable counts.
  • Grade Calculator — Add assignments with scores and weights to get your weighted final grade and letter.
  • Scientific Notation Converter — Convert between decimal numbers and scientific notation in both directions.
  • Multiplication Table Generator — Get the ×1 to ×12 multiplication table for any number from 1 to 20.
  • Study Planner — Spread your subjects and weekly study hours across Monday to Sunday.
  • Quiz Generator — Take a 10-question general knowledge quiz, one question at a time, with a score at the end.

No. Open any tool and start using it right away.
