How to Build a Free Resume Online

A clean, readable resume gets read. HecTool's Resume Builder turns a simple form into a tidy resume you can print or save as a PDF — free, with no account and no sign-up.

Step 1: Fill in your details

Open the Resume Builder and start with your name, phone number, email address and location. Add a short summary of two or three sentences: what you do, how much experience you have, and what you are good at. Keep it specific — a summary that could describe anyone helps no one.

Step 2: Add experience with evidence

List your jobs most recent first, with the job title, employer, dates and two to four bullet points each. Start bullets with action verbs such as built, improved, managed or delivered, and add numbers where you honestly can: sales grown, time saved, people served, projects shipped.

Step 3: Tighten the wording

Read every bullet aloud. If a sentence needs two breaths, shorten it. The AI Text Writer and AI Paraphraser can suggest tighter wording for a bullet you are stuck on — but check every suggestion, and never let it invent experience you do not have. Your resume must be true.

Step 4: Preview, proofread and adjust

Watch the live preview as you type. Aim for one page if you are early in your career, and two at most otherwise. Check spelling and grammar yourself, because the builder does not check them. Look for consistent dates, matching verb tenses, and no repeated words.

Step 5: Save it as a PDF

Use your browser's Print function and choose Save as PDF. Check the saved file opens correctly and that nothing is cut off at the edges. Name it clearly, such as firstname-lastname-resume.pdf. Your resume is not saved online, so keep the PDF; to edit later, return to the builder and fill the form again.

Tips for a stronger resume

Tailor the summary and bullets to each job you apply for, using words from the job advert where they honestly fit. Put the most relevant experience first. Keep formatting plain and consistent — fancy layouts distract from the content. Never include passwords or ID numbers on a resume.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to build a resume?

No. Fill in the form and use the preview right away, with no account and no sign-up.

Can I edit my resume after saving the PDF?

Yes — go back to the Resume Builder, update the form, and print or save as PDF again. The PDF itself is a finished file, so edits happen in the builder.

Is my resume stored online?

No. The tool runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or saved online. Keep the PDF you save, and copy any text you want to reuse before you leave the page.
