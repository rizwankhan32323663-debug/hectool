Date & Time — 6 free online tools

Six tools that answer time questions exactly: the days between two dates, how long until an event, the same moment in another time zone, live clocks for major zones, plus a stopwatch and a countdown timer with a finish alert. Handy for deadlines, travel, exams and events — no calendar flipping required.

  • Stopwatch — Stopwatch with start, stop, lap times and reset.
  • Countdown Timer — Count down from any minutes and seconds, with a finish alert.
  • World Clock — Live clocks for UTC, Pakistan, EST, GMT, IST and JST.
  • Days Between Dates — Count the exact days between two dates.
  • Days Until Date — How many days until (or since) any date.
  • Time Zone Converter — Convert a time between time zones with the Intl API.
